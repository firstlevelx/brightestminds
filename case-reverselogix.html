Case study · ReverseLogix · Returns‑management SaaS

216 interested leads and 108 booked demos

Senior transportation executives across North America are notoriously hard to engage. Clean targeting and non‑salesy messaging booked 108 demos.

The interesting part

Messages were deliberately non‑salesy, built around the specific day‑to‑day pain points of transportation executives, and they still booked 108 demos.

The client

ReverseLogix is a SaaS company specializing in product returns management, helping businesses automate complex returns, cut costs and improve customer satisfaction.

The challenge

  • Hard‑to‑reach audience. Senior executives in transportation departments across North America.
  • Past outreach. Earlier attempts had damaged reach and response.

What we did

  • Clean prospect lists. Rebuilt the target list around the ideal customer and verified every contact.
  • Non‑salesy messaging. Personalized outreach around the specific pain points of transportation leaders.

The results

  • 108 booked demos with senior decision‑makers.
  • 216 interested leads plus 183 “maybe later” replies.
  • 13% response rate across the campaign.
